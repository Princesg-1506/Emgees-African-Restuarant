Our Popular Dishes

Discover our handcrafted traditional Nigerian dishes, made with the finest ingredients and authentic recipes. Each creation combines delightful textures and flavors for a truly West African experience you won't forget.

Meat Pies

Our signature Nigerian meat pies are Edmonton's best! Flaky, golden pastry filled with perfectly seasoned ground beef, potatoes, and aromatic spices. A beloved West African snack that's crispy on the outside and deliciously savory on the inside. Perfect as an appetizer or satisfying snack any time of day. Customers agree - these are a MUST TRY!

Jollof Rice

The iconic West African rice dish that's seriously amazing! Our Jollof Rice is cooked to perfection in a rich blend of tomatoes, peppers, and traditional spices, creating layers of smoky, savory flavor. Available with your choice of chicken, beef, fish, or vegetarian. This beloved Nigerian staple is a customer favorite that captures authentic home-cooked taste in every bite.

Efo Riro

A traditional Nigerian vegetable stew that's pure comfort food! Made with fresh leafy greens, tomatoes, peppers, and your choice of protein, creating a rich, hearty stew full of authentic flavor. This beloved Nigerian dish represents authentic home cooking and is a favorite among our customers seeking genuine West African flavors. Served with rice, fufu, or pounded yam.

Seafood Peppered Rice

A flavorful Nigerian-style rice dish that brings authentic coastal cooking to Edmonton. Perfectly seasoned rice with fresh seafood, peppers, and aromatic spices prepared in traditional West African style. This hearty dish is a favorite among customers seeking genuine Nigerian home-style cooking. Each bite captures the essence of authentic African culinary traditions with a delightful seafood twist.

Fried Rice

An authentic Nigerian-style fried rice that represents traditional home cooking at its finest. Perfectly cooked rice stir-fried with vegetables, spices, and your choice of protein, creating a flavorful and satisfying dish. This beloved Nigerian staple showcases the depth and complexity of West African cuisine and is a favorite among those who appreciate genuine African flavors.

Yam Porridge

A beloved Nigerian comfort food that's hearty, flavorful, and deeply satisfying. Made with fresh yam, vegetables, and traditional spices, creating a rich and creamy porridge that's perfect for any meal. This traditional dish captures the authentic taste that reminds customers of home. A must-try for anyone exploring Nigerian cuisine and seeking genuine West African comfort food!
